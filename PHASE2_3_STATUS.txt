💡 HOW IT WORKS
──────────────

Loop (Backward Jump):
  Before (3 bytes): OP_LOOP [u16 offset]
  After (2 bytes):  OP_LOOP_SHORT [i8 offset]
  Savings: 1 byte per loop

Forward Jumps: Not yet optimized (requires two-pass or 
peephole optimization to avoid bytecode shifting complexity)
